# Crypto day trading

- Best conditions: weekdays during European and US hours (about 07:00–20:00 UTC), with the 1-hour trend up and price holding above VWAP.
- Enter on a pullback or breakout that's confirmed by a 5-minute candle close and rising volume. Skip choppy, sideways sessions.
- Stops: about 1.5–2.5x the recent 1-hour ATR (often about 1–2% for BTC, 4–8% for meme coins). Target at least 2x the stop, and at least 1.5% for blue chips.
- Keep total meme coin exposure small (one position at a time) and skip meme coins in thin hours.
- After a sharp move larger than about 3x ATR, or when spreads look wide, wait for things to settle.
- Stop for the day after about 2% total loss; don't make up losses with bigger trades.
