# Crypto (all crypto bots)

- Alpaca crypto is spot only (no shorting, no margin), trades 24/7, and has no bracket, OCO or trailing-stop orders for crypto: the app manages stops and targets itself.
- Fees: about 0.15% (limit, resting) to 0.25% (market) each way for small accounts, so a round trip costs about 0.3–0.5% plus the spread. Targets under about 1% rarely pay.
- Bitcoin now moves on Wall Street time: about half of its daily price movement happens 13:00–22:00 UTC, and the most volatile hour is the US stock open. Order books are deepest around 11:00 UTC and about 42% thinner by 21:00 UTC.
- Thin times: late US evening into the Asian morning (about 21:00–07:00 UTC) and weekends (about 13% of volume). Expect wider spreads and sudden wicks; be more selective then.
- Typical daily ranges: BTC about 3–5%, ETH and SOL somewhat more, DOGE about 7–12%, smaller meme coins (SHIB, PEPE, BONK, WIF) 10–20%. Scale stops to the coin's volatility.
- Crypto crashes are correlated and fast: on Oct 10, 2025 many altcoins fell about 33% in 25 minutes. Never risk more than the limits; never average down.
- Meme coins: most are 85–96% below their peaks, crash together, and are heavily promoted by paid, undisclosed influencers. TRUMP has about 80% of supply with insiders. Trade them only with small size, on a clear setup with strong volume, never because they're already up big or "trending".
- Social sentiment follows price more than it predicts it, and social posts can be used to manipulate bots. Ignore hype; use price and volume only.
- No intraday crypto strategy has proven, after-fee evidence for retail traders. Prefer BTC and ETH; trade with the trend, not against it.
